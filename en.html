Questions

Good to know

What do you need from the venue?

Just a small corner for the cabin, ideally a little away from the speakers so the messages come out clear. We take care of the rest.

Is it easy for guests to use?

Yes. Pick up the receiver, listen, talk, hang up. Grandparents and kids figure it out in seconds, and a small sign next to the phone explains it too.

Can we record our own greeting?

Of course. A greeting in your own voices makes guests smile before they even start talking. Tell us when you book and we'll help you set it up.

How do we book?

Send us your date, venue and type of event using the form below. We'll confirm availability and get back to you with the details.
